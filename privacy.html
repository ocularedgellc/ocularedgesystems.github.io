Privacy Policy for SpellReady: Spelling List

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ocular Edge LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in SpellReady: Spelling List (“SpellReady”).

Summary

SpellReady does not require an account. The current Android version has no Ocular Edge account, cloud-sync, advertising, or analytics service. Spelling lists and practice progress are stored in the app on your device. Android system backup and the device’s text-to-speech service may handle some information as described below.

Information stored by the app

The app may store on your device:

  • Spelling lists, words, and related text entered into the app.
  • Practice progress for those words.
  • Parent-recorded pronunciation clips associated with words.
  • Parent-access information, including a PIN verifier, recovery-code information, and—if set—an optional PIN hint. The hint is stored as readable text and is shown on the locked screen only when someone taps to reveal it.

Microphone and parent recordings

SpellReady uses Android’s microphone permission only when someone enters Parent Mode and explicitly starts a word-recording action. The recording is saved in the app’s private storage and is used to play that word’s pronunciation. SpellReady does not continuously record audio or transcribe speech. Anyone speaking near the device while recording is active may be captured.

Text-to-speech

When bundled audio or a parent recording is unavailable, SpellReady may pass a word and its associated sentence to the text-to-speech engine selected on the Android device. The engine may synthesize speech on-device or use a network service, depending on the selected engine, voice, and device settings. That processing is governed by the engine provider’s privacy practices. Ocular Edge does not receive these requests through an Ocular Edge server.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include app preferences and files in its backup or device-transfer process. This could include app data such as lists, progress, parent-access settings, or recordings. Android and Google control that service and its settings; Ocular Edge does not operate or control your device backup. Check your Android and Google account backup settings if you want to manage or disable backups.

How information is used and shared

SpellReady uses locally stored information to provide its spelling, progress, parent-access, and audio features. Ocular Edge does not receive app data automatically through an Ocular Edge account or sync service, and we do not sell app data. Information may be handled by Android backup or the selected text-to-speech provider as described above. If you contact us, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Children’s privacy

SpellReady is designed for spelling practice by children with parent involvement. The app does not require a child account or ask for a child’s name or email address. Parents can enter words and other text into lists, so please avoid entering sensitive personal information. Microphone recording is a parent-controlled feature and is active only while a recording is being made.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on the device until it is deleted in SpellReady or the app’s storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Parents can remove lists, words, and associated parent recordings through the app. Android backups may retain or restore app data according to the device’s backup settings, even after local data is removed.

Security

SpellReady stores its local data in Android app storage. The app stores a verifier for the parent PIN rather than the PIN itself; an optional PIN hint is stored in readable form. Device security and Android backup settings are managed by the device owner and Android.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Ocular Edge LLC at: support@ocularedgesystems.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.
